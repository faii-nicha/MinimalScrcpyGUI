## 📡 Setting Up Your Android Device for Mirroring

MinimalScrcpyGUI needs permission to access your Android device. Here’s how to prepare your phone or tablet:

1. On your Android device, open **Settings**.
2. Scroll to **About phone** (or **About device**) at the bottom.
3. Find **Build number**.
4. Tap **Build number** seven times quickly.
5. You will see a message that Developer Options are enabled.
6. Go back to **Settings** and open **Developer options**.
7. Enable **USB debugging**.
8. If you prefer wireless mirroring, make sure your phone and PC are on the same Wi-Fi network.

## 🔗 Connecting Your Phone to Your PC

MinimalScrcpyGUI supports two connection methods:

### Method 1: USB Connection

1. Plug your Android device into your PC with a USB cable.
2. Run MinimalScrcpyGUI on your PC.
3. The app should detect your device automatically.
4. Confirm on your Android device if it asks to allow USB debugging.
5. Your Android screen will appear in the app window.

### Method 2: Wireless Connection

1. Connect your Android device and Windows PC to the same Wi-Fi network.
2. Connect your device with USB once to set up the wireless connection.
3. Open MinimalScrcpyGUI on your PC.
4. Click on the **Wireless** button inside the app.
5. The app will connect to your device over Wi-Fi.
6. After successful connection, you can unplug the USB cable.
7. Your Android screen will be mirrored wirelessly.

## ⚙️ Troubleshooting Common Issues

If the app does not connect or show your phone screen, try the following:

- Check that USB debugging is enabled on your Android device.
- For wireless use, make sure both devices are on the same Wi-Fi network.
- Restart your PC and Android device.
- Use a different USB cable or USB port.
- Verify MinimalScrcpyGUI is allowed through your Windows firewall.
- Close other applications that might use ADB or the Android device.
- Reinstall the app if problems persist.
